## 1. Verdict

Ready after fixes; not ready as it stands. The verdicts themselves are sound and stated without
softening: every miss opens "The criterion is missed", the re-baseline is a re-baseline, and the
collision claim keeps the locked form. The problems lie around the verdicts:

- One sentence contradicts the *Mémoire de Master* on why the end-to-end budget was re-baselined.
- Three of the located causes go further than the measurement:
  - the reflex delay rests on an energy-based anchor that some detections precede by up to 235 ms;
  - the recovery cause comes from server configuration, not from a measurement;
  - the safe-failure comparison is against a baseline from a different population.
- The acoustic statistics leave out two things that decide how "not significant at p = 0.0051"
  and the intervals should be read: which McNemar form was used, and that the 200 items are
  paraphrases of 12 patterns.
