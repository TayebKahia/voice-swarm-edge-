## Read before quoting

- **NFR-16 is FRR <= 0.10.** A class meets it on this evidence only if its whole interval is below the budget; at n = 20 that needs zero misses and still fails (upper bound ~0.17). Quote the point estimate *with* its interval.
- Missed takes, with the peak score their own head reached: `swarm_hold`: w03 (0.9977); `swarm_abort`: none. The threshold is 0.999, at the top of the grid (`results/wake_training.md`: the scores saturate), so a miss can sit a hair under it. Per-take peaks are in `results/wake_real_voice.json`.
- Delay is measured from the end of speech as the capture tool recorded it (`session_meta.json` tail silence), not from a forced alignment; it is indicative, and NFR-1's latency is Exp-2's measurement on the Pi.
- One speaker in one quiet room: this closes the *synthetic-voice* caveat, not the speaker or noise ones.
