## 1. Verdict on D5

No citation fails its sentence: of 10 sites, 8 are SUPPORTS and 2 PARTIAL. There is no DOES NOT SUPPORT
and no COULD NOT ACCESS. Both paper numbers (680,000 hours; 39 M smallest) and "encoder-decoder
Transformer" are in the Whisper paper verbatim. The main gaps are uncited claims that the seven
`\TODO`s do not cover. The flight-controller paragraph (loop rates, setpoint interfaces) and the
claim that minimising total squared distance avoids crossing paths are the two a jury will ask
about (MAJOR). Several other uncited sentences could be carried at once by keys already in
references.bib (`whisper`, `speechcmd`, `pyflyt`, `oww`, `silero`, `qin2021`, `llamacpp`). Two
PARTIALs need rewording: Reynolds' "central force" and Vaswani as the source of a language-model
definition.
