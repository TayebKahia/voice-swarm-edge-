# Argument review: thesis/ingenieur/ch1_introduction.tex (Ingénieur Ch1)

Dimensions covered: D1, D2, D4, D6, D10. Out of scope, owned by another agent: D5, D7, D8, D9, D11, D12. D3 was done by the script.

## 1. Verdict

The chapter is ready once the fixes below are made. It is not ready as it stands. Its structure, ownership discipline and NFR-12 wording are sound. Three parts of the safety argument do not hold:

- **Branch A validation.** l.224-235 says Branch A skips all three validation layers. Ch3 (l.157-160) and `swarm/fsm.py:165` both show the opposite.
- **Single-path argument.** "No single path can satisfy both" budgets (l.126-133) is a non sequitur. A path that meets 150 ms automatically meets 2,500 ms.
- **Rejection outcome.** "Every rejection at every layer resolves to a hold" (l.196-201) is contradicted by ADR-0002, whose precise form Ch3 has already committed to stating.

Several promises also point at the wrong chapter, or at a requirement the chapter never names. The main cases are the clamp (Ch3 vs Ch4) and the "degrades safely" clause of RQ3.
