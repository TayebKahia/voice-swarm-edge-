## 3. New problems found while verifying (NEW — not verified by a second pass)

1. **NEW, bears on C3 and C2's fixes.** Both reviewer fixes contain errors; see the table. C3's
   rewording is contradicted by `data/test_golden.jsonl` and `data/train.jsonl`. C2's fix attributes
   `train_wake.py`'s 1.975 s window to openWakeWord.
2. **NEW, MINOR (D2).** l.38 says "The two paths share nothing between the audio buffer and the bus".
   In the code, the endpointer and the spotter run one after the other on the same thread for every
   frame (`runtime/stream.py:169-172`), so the endpointer's per-frame compute delays the spotter.
   The dataflow is separate, but the execution is shared. Suggested: "share no data between…", or add
   "apart from the frame loop of Section~\ref{sec:resource-allocation}".
3. **NEW, MINOR (D2, related to A2/A3).** A spoken "swarm hold" or "swarm abort" is also endpointed
   and queued for the parse path. Nothing after a trigger suppresses it (`stream.py:172-175`). Its
   parse-path result carries a later sequence number and is dispatched. A mis-parse of the stopping
   phrase could therefore take effect after the reflex hold. The chapter does not say what the parse
   path does with a phrase the reflex path already acted on.
4. **NEW, NIT.** C1's evidence cites `runtime/pipeline.py:153` for the Pi-side validation. That line
   is a docstring; the call is at l.200.
5. **NEW, for the Master reviewer.** `thesis/master/ch3_method.tex:97-98` makes the same
   "GBNF cannot express a range" claim as C4. The Master's own l.103-104 describes `idlist` as
   restricting identifiers to 0--4, which is a range expressed in GBNF.
