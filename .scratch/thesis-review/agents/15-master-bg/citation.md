## 5. UNVERIFIED

- **mcnemar1947 and dunn1961 full text.** Both records were confirmed via Crossref (and the Cambridge landing page for McNemar), but the bodies are paywalled (Springer/Cambridge) or blocked (tandfonline HTTP 403). The claims attached to them are the textbook content of those papers; I could not quote the page.
- **"fewer and narrower blocks" (line 91).** The SmolLM2 paper gives the 1.7B architecture only (Table 6: 24 layers, 2,048 width) and says the 360M "share[s] the same architecture ... but use[s] Grouped Query Attention". Whether the 360M has fewer layers than the 1.7B is not stated in the paper (its model card would say). As a generic definition of an SLM against a tens-of-billions LLM the sentence is textbook-level; as a claim sourced to smollm2 it is PARTIAL.
- **"no accelerator a language model runtime can use" (line 218-219).** The Pi 5 has a VideoCore VII GPU with Vulkan 1.2 (product brief) and llama.cpp lists a Vulkan backend (README build table). Whether that backend runs on the Pi 5, and usefully, was not checked; the sentence may need "no accelerator this work's runtime uses" or a source.
- **Chat-template date note in the `llama32card` bib entry.** The model-card page does not show the date-in-system-prompt behaviour; it lives in the tokenizer configuration file. Not a chapter claim; flagged only so the note is not mistaken for a card quotation.
- **Bib venue for `lora`**: confirmed on iclr.cc (ICLR 2022 poster); openreview was behind a verification wall. `gptq` ICLR 2023 and `qin2021` IJCAI 2021 confirmed from the arXiv comments lines; `koo2024` COLM 2024 from the PDF header; `spqr` ICLR 2024 and `awq` MLSys 2024 from the proceedings pages the bib links; `qlora` NeurIPS 36 from Crossref. No other D5e discrepancy found in the 23 entries.
