## 1. Verdict

**Ready after fixes; no BLOCKERs.** The document builds cleanly: 0 `^!` errors, 0 undefined citations and no `Float too large`. Every one of the 10 cited keys resolves to an entry whose title, authors and year match the primary page. Most citations support the sentence they carry.

The chapter needs four fixes:
- **Three citations over-reach.**
  - `pyflyt` at l.48 sits on a claim about this system's own simulation setup.
  - `avizienis2004` at l.198 is credited with a definition of "fail-safe" that the taxonomy does not give in that form.
  - `stpa` at l.181 is cited for "standing practice ... not a departure", although the handbook presents STPA as a departure from probabilistic hazard analysis.
- **Undefined codes (D11 FAILs).** Branch A/B and Exp-2/3/4 appear 25 times, all but two inside the two requirement tables, and are never defined. Issue 00 already decides the replacement names.
- **Presentation fixes are mechanical but numerous.** They cover about 20 acronym sites and about 9 rhetorical flourishes.
